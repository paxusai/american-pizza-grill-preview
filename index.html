Our story

A Beach Road original.

This corner of Garapan has been feeding Saipan since 1990, first as Bobby Cadillac’s and today as American Pizza & Grill.

It’s a modern Italian-American kitchen. Pizzas are built by hand on the line, burgers are certified Angus on brioche buns baked in house, and steaks, ribs and fried chicken are made to order. Behind the bar there’s a cocktail list created with Cha Cafe next door.
